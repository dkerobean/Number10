# Page Topology

## Reference Site Sections
- Header: floating navigation with brand, uppercase links, booking CTA.
- Home hero: eyebrow, dominant headline, address/hours/phone blocks, booking CTA.
- Testimonial: quote-led trust section.
- About preview: large editorial heading, paragraph, CTA, portrait image.
- Services preview: grouped service category cards with browse CTA.
- Gallery preview: bold heading and two large grooming images.
- Footer: navigation, social links, address, directions, hours, newsletter input, phone, booking CTA.

## Number10 Demo Routes
- `/`: homepage with hero, trust, about preview, service preview, YouTube video, shop preview, gallery preview.
- `/services`: service categories and pricing.
- `/about`: story, team, standards, testimonials.
- `/gallery`: masonry image grid.
- `/contact`: booking form and contact details.
- `/shop`: razor blade ecommerce demo.
